# Your body weight and the adaptive calorie target

I am 16 or older, and I agree that Haki stores the body weights I enter
and uses them, together with the food I log, to estimate how much energy
I use and to suggest a daily calorie target.

This is health data. Haki uses it only for this, shows it to no one else,
and never sells or shares it.

I can withdraw this consent at any time in Profile. Haki then deletes my
weigh-ins and my estimates at once; I can download all my data first.

Haki works without this consent: I can still log food and set my own
calorie target.
